# Elements of AI: Free AI Course Now Offering University Credits 🎓

*Published: June 24 2025*

I'm excited to share a valuable resource for anyone interested in learning about AI - [Elements of AI](https://www.elementsofai.se/), a free online course that now offers the opportunity to earn university credits through Linköping University in Sweden.

## Two Courses to Choose From

Elements of AI offers two comprehensive courses:

### 1. Introduction to AI
- 15-30 hours of content
- No programming required
- 6 chapters covering fundamentals
- Available completely free on the platform
- Optional university credits available

### 2. Building AI
- 50 hours of content
- Flexible difficulty levels
- From no-code to Python programming
- Practical implementation focus
- Free with optional certificate purchase

## Course Content

### Introduction to AI Chapters:
1. What is artificial intelligence?
2. Problem-solving with AI
3. AI in practice
4. Machine learning
5. Neural networks
6. Implications and future of AI

### Building AI Chapters:
1. Getting started with AI
2. Dealing with uncertainty
3. Machine learning
4. Neural networks
5. Conclusion & Your AI project

## Flexible Learning Options

You can choose how you want to engage with the courses:

1. **Free Platform Access**
   - Access all content directly on [Elements of AI platform](https://course.elementsofai.com/se/)
   - Learn at your own pace
   - No formal requirements
   - Available in multiple languages

2. **University Credits Option** (For Introduction to AI)
   - Course Code: ETE318
   - Credits: 2 hp
   - Apply through Linköping University
   - Cost:
     - Free for EU/EES students
     - 4700 SEK for non-EU/EES students
   - Requires basic university eligibility
   - Flexible start dates throughout 2025

## How to Get Started

### For Free Platform Access:
1. Visit [Elements of AI](https://course.elementsofai.com/se/)
2. Choose your preferred course
3. Start learning immediately

### For University Credits:
1. Complete the Introduction to AI course on the platform
2. Get your course certificate
3. Apply through [Linköping University's course page](https://liu.se/utbildning/kurs/ete318)
4. Upload your certificate and complete the validation test
