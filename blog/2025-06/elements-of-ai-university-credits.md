# Elements of AI: Free AI Course Now Offering University Credits 🎓

*Published: June 24 2025*  
*Author: Lucy Sonberg*  
*Reading Time: 8 minutes*

> **Summary**: Discover how Elements of AI, a free online course collaboration between Linköping University and Helsinki University, is making AI education accessible to everyone. Learn about their two comprehensive courses and how you can earn university credits while studying AI fundamentals.

In the rapidly evolving world of artificial intelligence, finding high-quality, accessible education can be challenging. That's why I'm thrilled to share an exceptional resource that's breaking down barriers to AI education: [Elements of AI](https://www.elementsofai.se/). This initiative, born from a collaboration between leading institutions, is revolutionizing how we learn about AI - and now offers the opportunity to earn university credits through Linköping University in Sweden.

## A Tale of Two Courses

The Elements of AI program has evolved into something truly special, offering two distinct learning paths that cater to different needs and skill levels. Let me walk you through what makes each course unique and valuable.

### Introduction to AI: Your First Step into the World of AI

The first course, Introduction to AI, is designed with accessibility in mind. Taking just 15-30 hours to complete, it opens the door to AI understanding for everyone - regardless of their technical background. What makes this course particularly special is its dual-track approach: you can either take it for free on the platform, learning at your own pace, or pursue it for university credits through Linköping University.

The course takes you through six fascinating chapters:
- Beginning with the fundamental question of "What is artificial intelligence?"
- Exploring how AI approaches problem-solving
- Examining AI in practical applications
- Diving into the basics of machine learning
- Understanding neural networks
- Contemplating the implications and future of AI

### Building AI: Taking Your Knowledge Further

For those ready to dive deeper, the Building AI course offers an innovative approach to learning. What sets this course apart is its flexibility - you can engage with the content at different levels, from no-code approaches to hands-on Python programming. Over approximately 50 hours, you'll explore:

- Getting started with practical AI implementation
- Understanding uncertainty in AI systems
- Exploring machine learning in depth
- Working with neural networks
- Creating your own AI project

## A Flexible Approach to Learning

What truly excites me about this program is its commitment to accessibility and flexibility. Whether you're a busy professional, a student, or simply curious about AI, there's a path for you.

### Learning Your Way

The program offers two main approaches:

For self-paced learners, the platform provides complete freedom to learn at your own speed, with all materials available free of charge. This is perfect for those looking to build their knowledge without formal commitments.

For those seeking academic recognition, the Introduction to AI course can be taken through Linköping University, leading to 2 higher education credits (hp). This option includes:
- Flexible start dates throughout 2025
- Distance learning format
- Validation of your learning through a test
- Official university recognition

### Cost Structure

Transparency in education is crucial, so let's be clear about the costs:
- The platform courses are completely free for everyone
- University credits through Linköping University are:
  - Free for EU/EES students
  - 4700 SEK for non-EU/EES students

## Getting Started

Ready to begin your AI learning journey? Here's how to get started:

If you're interested in the free platform version:
1. Visit [Elements of AI](https://course.elementsofai.com/se/)
2. Choose your preferred course
3. Start learning at your own pace

For those interested in university credits:
1. Complete the Introduction to AI course on the platform
2. Obtain your course certificate
3. Apply through [Linköping University's course page](https://liu.se/utbildning/kurs/ete318)
4. Submit your certificate and complete the validation test
