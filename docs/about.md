# About Learn Agentic AI with Me

This project is dedicated to exploring and documenting the fascinating world of Agentic AI - artificial intelligence systems that can act autonomously. Our focus is on understanding how AI agents can perceive their environment, make decisions, and take actions independently, while maintaining reliability and ethical considerations.

## What is Agentic AI?

Agentic AI refers to AI systems that can:
* Act autonomously on behalf of users
* Understand and interpret complex instructions
* Make decisions based on context and goals
* Learn from interactions and feedback
* Collaborate with humans and other AI agents
* Adapt to new situations and environments

## What You'll Find Here

* **News Updates**: Latest developments in Agentic AI and autonomous systems
* **Blog Posts**: Deep dives into AI agent capabilities and applications
* **Resources**: Curated learning materials about AI agents and autonomy
* **Projects**: Practical experiments with autonomous AI systems
* **Tutorials**: Step-by-step guides for working with AI agents
* **Research**: Summaries of important papers in Agentic AI
